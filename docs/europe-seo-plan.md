# PREJ — Europe B2B organic search plan

Updated: 2026-11-04. Website: https://prej-tech.com/

## Positioning and limits
PREJ SH.P.K. is based in Mitrovica, Kosovo and offers custom software development to businesses across Europe. The site does **not** claim offices or staffed teams elsewhere, does **not** invent testimonials, and does **not** imply certifications or completed client work that cannot be documented. No guaranteed Google rankings.

The English homepage **/** is canonical, while **/en/** is a duplicate presentation with canonical pointing to **/**. Separate localized pages use **/sq/**, **/de/**, **/it/** and corresponding service URLs. The existing reciprocal hreflang mapping, x-default, robots.txt and 20-URL sitemap remain in place. Do not make every translation canonical to the homepage.

## Geographic reach by language
- English: general European B2B audience; includes UK/Ireland, Nordics, Benelux and buyers who search in English elsewhere.
- German: Germany, Austria, German-speaking Switzerland.
- Italian: Italy and Italian-speaking Switzerland.
- Albanian: Kosovo, Albania and Albanian-speaking businesses in European diaspora.
- Other European markets: English content can be discovered, but reliable rankings for French-, Spanish-, Dutch-, Polish- and other language-specific queries normally require complete, reviewed local-language pages. Expand only after demand and professional translation are established.

## Next editorial work, prioritized
1. Publish factual case studies with permission: problem, workflow, scope, stack, current deployment state, screenshots from the real product, and outcomes supported by actual evidence. Never invent numbers or client endorsements.
2. Expand the strongest service pages with buyer FAQs based on real enquiries: how scoping works, MVP versus production release, existing-system access, timelines, maintenance, data ownership and deployment responsibilities. Avoid generic AI-generated filler.

6. Revisit performance: image sizing, loading, interaction delays and Core Web Vitals on real phones. Keep all content readable without JavaScript.

## Measurement
Use Google Search Console **Performance → Search results** to export the baseline: last 28 days, then monthly comparisons for clicks, impressions, CTR, average position and countries. Segment by:
- Search query cluster (software, web systems, mobile, API).
- Landing page (homepage, service, case study).
- Country and language, where available.

Website enquiries currently open an email draft; the site does not have analytics conversion events. Do not report enquiry rates or leads as measured until a privacy-reviewed tracking method is implemented.

Monitor index coverage in **Pages**, performance in **Search results**, and **Core Web Vitals** separately. Sitemap success means URL discovery, not guaranteed indexing. A submitted URL may take time to be crawled and still may not index.

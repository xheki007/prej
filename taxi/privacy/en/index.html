PREJ TAXI & PREJ DRIVER

Privacy for PREJ applications

How PREJ Taxi for passengers and PREJ Driver for drivers, provided by PREJ SH.P.K., use data.

PrivacyAccount deletionHelp
Draft for review before release

This document describes features verified during development. Final retention periods, the erasure procedure and production service providers are being verified. It is not yet the final privacy policy for Google Play publication.

Data used by the service

An account uses a phone number, display name and account identifier. Sign-in verification and device sessions help protect access.

For a trip, the service processes pickup and destination, time, assigned driver, status, fare, payment and receipt. If you complete a billing profile, it also processes the information you provide for that profile.

For drivers, the service may process applications, documents submitted for verification, vehicles and fleet membership. Device information, installation identifiers, notification tokens and security logs support the service. The final inventory depends on the features enabled in the released version.

Location and permissions

PREJ Taxi uses location while you use the app, with your permission, to set a pickup point. You can also choose an address manually. The passenger app does not require background location tracking.

PREJ Driver uses location for nearby offers, trip assignment and arrival updates. With separate permission, location may also be sent while the app is in the background or closed, while you are online or on an active trip. Tracking behavior and stopping are checked in final release tests.

You can manage location and notification permissions in your device settings. Refusing them limits features that depend on those permissions. Uninstalling the app does not delete the account or server-side history.

Who receives data

Passengers and drivers see the information needed for their trip. Authorized operator or fleet personnel have role-based access for dispatch, support and administration.

Technical services for hosting, maps, message verification and notifications may process data to provide their respective features. The provider inventory and production configuration must be confirmed before release. The code includes Google Maps, Expo Notifications and support for Twilio Verify; this alone does not establish that every provider is active in production.

Retention and deletion

Account and trip data are stored in the PREJ service. Final periods for history, driver documents, receipts, logs and backups have not yet been approved in this draft.

Account deletion must include associated data that no longer has a documented reason for retention. Any exception for financial records, security or legal obligations must have a purpose, retention period and restricted access; disabling sign-in alone is not deletion.

Data questions and requests

You can contact PREJ SH.P.K. for information, corrections or a request to delete your account and associated data. Use the Account deletion page above or the contact below.

Provide only the minimum information needed to identify your account. Do not email passwords, verification codes, identity documents or payment-card details. Account ownership is verified separately before any deletion.

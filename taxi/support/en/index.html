Two applications, clear roles

PREJ Taxi is the application for requesting and following a trip as a passenger. PREJ Driver is for drivers with activated access, trip offers and trip management.

The applications are being prepared for release. This page does not announce general Google Play availability, city coverage or guaranteed service hours.

Tell us what happened

Contact the email address below with the app, version, device model and a brief description of the issue. Before sending a screenshot, remove names, phone numbers, precise addresses and payment information.

Do not send passwords or verification codes. For data requests, use the privacy and account deletion pages.

Fare and payment

Check the payment method and fare on the relevant trip screen. Test materials verify the cash payment flow. Card payments or an electronic wallet should not be considered available unless shown as activated in the relevant service.

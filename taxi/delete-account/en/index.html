PREJ TAXI & PREJ DRIVER

Request account deletion

Send a request concerning your account and associated data in PREJ Taxi or PREJ Driver. You do not need to sign in to the app.

PrivacyAccount deletionHelp

What to include

State the app, the phone number linked to your account, and whether you request deletion of the entire account and associated data or only specific data. If you use both apps with the same number, say whether the request applies to both.

What happens next

The approved period for deleting ordinary account data is 30 days after account ownership is verified. The retention period for invoices and financial receipts will be confirmed separately. The execution procedure and final policy are still being prepared before release.

PREJ SH.P.K. reviews the request and verifies account ownership before taking action. The data to be deleted and any records that must be retained for a documented reason will be clarified. A reply or disabled sign-in is not confirmation that deletion has been completed.

Your profile, device identifiers and associated account data are included in the request review. Receipts and financial records may have separate retention requirements. Final periods and the execution procedure are still being prepared before release; see the privacy draft. This page does not perform automatic deletion.

Do not send sensitive information

Do not include passwords, SMS/OTP codes, photographs of identity documents or payment-card numbers. Provide only your account phone number and request. Do not send another person's data.

Prepare an email request

The button only opens a draft in your email application. Review and send it yourself. This page does not send email, collect its contents or confirm deletion.
